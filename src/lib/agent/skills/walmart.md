# Walmart listing skill

Walmart's search reads the title, description and key features, and titles get cut off after about 50 characters in results.

## Fields and limits
- title: up to 150 characters, aim for 50 to 75. Brand, product line, defining quality, item name, size. Put the important words in the first 50 characters.
- shortDescription: up to 500 characters. What it is and why it matters.
- bullets (key features): 5 to 7, each up to 80 characters. They get truncated beyond that. No ending punctuation.
- description: plain text, at least 150 words and up to 4,000 characters. No HTML.

## Rules
- No ALL CAPS, special characters (™, @, *), promotional phrases, competitor names or subjective rankings like "best" or "top rated".
- No URLs, contact details or time-sensitive information.
- Don't repeat keywords excessively.
- Sellers must be able to validate every product or performance claim.
