# verda.com product page skill

This is our own site, so it's written for search engines and for answer engines that quote pages directly.

## Fields and limits
- title: the product name as shoppers would say it, up to 70 characters.
- metaTitle: up to 60 characters. Product and its main benefit.
- metaDescription: up to 155 characters. A specific reason to click.
- bullets: 3 to 5 short benefits.
- description: open with one plain sentence saying what it is, who it's for and what makes it different. Answer engines quote this sentence. 80 to 200 words in VERDA's voice.
- faq: 3 to 5 real buyer questions with direct answers of 1 to 3 sentences. Start each answer with the answer. Only state facts in the product data. Questions like "How do I use it?" and "What's in it?" work well.

## Rules
- Everything visible must match the product's structured data exactly, since these fields also fill its Product JSON-LD.
- Use VERDA's voice: concise, plain, ingredient-forward, never promotional.
- No drug or medical claims. Only approved claims.
